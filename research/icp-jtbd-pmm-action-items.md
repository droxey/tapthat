**Date:** 2026-09-20  
**Status:** execution list pulled from the memo. Not a new strategy.

Highest leverage first: **$10 event single + couple kit + host seeding + activate/second-chapter instrumentation.** Everything else waits on those four.

---

## Price and SKU

1. Cut the event-table single to **$10**. Leave the DTC 10-pack at **$39**.
2. Add a **couple kit at $18** (two discs, “one tap, two vetoes”).
3. Keep house / 100+ at **$3–4 per unit**. Do not publish $10 as the wholesale number.
4. Rewrite the single vs pack frame: **$10 is the one on the bracelet. The pack is for the night.**

---

## Product and app

5. Do not subscription-gate the first tap.
6. Make **public/private + couple veto** the advertised feature, not a FAQ footnote.
7. Print Android tap instructions in the bag (mid-back, unlocked home screen).
8. Lead privacy copy with **no GPS, no battery, link opens only on choice**.
9. Instrument: taps per disc per night, activate rate, couple-linked profiles, second-chapter rate, 10-pack attach, repurchase.

## Copy

## GTM — 30 days

13. Week 0: put $10 on **event one-pagers only**. Site pack stays $39.
14. Week 1: seed **20 host 10-packs** across the next 5 ticker events.
15. Week 2: ship the couple-kit landing. No feature work.
16. Week 3: judge on **activate ≥40% of taps** and **second chapter ≥15%**. Below that, treat the object as jewelry and stop network spend.
17. Consign at embarkation / villa check-in / pool bar. $10 cash or tap-to-pay.
18. Pay **20% affiliate** on couple kits for travel agents and opted-in event chats.
19. No TikTok “what the pineapple means” explainers.

## Do not

## Kill criteria

If activation stays under 40% of taps, or a second chapter is written on under 15% of activated charms, the bead is jewelry. Stop treating it as a network until those numbers move.
