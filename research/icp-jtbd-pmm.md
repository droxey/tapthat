Lifestyle travel already solved *signal* (upside-down pineapple jewelry, black rings, Bliss Cruise color-bead name necklaces) and *search* (SDC, Kasidie, Feeld ~$12–30/mo, 3Fun). It did **not** solve the 20-second pool-deck moment:

## ICP

### Primary — Weekend Couple (60% of $10 unit volume)

| Field | Spec |
|---|---|
| Who | Married or partnered MF couple, 32–55, in the lifestyle 1–8 years |
| Money | $4k–12k/year on travel + events. Cabin $2.3k–8k. Jewelry already in the bag. |
| Where | Desire / Hedonism / Temptation, hotel takeovers, Bliss-class charters, Miami/Vegas pool circuit, Palm Springs pineapple weekends |
| Stack | SDC or Kasidie (rusty), Feeld for cities, group chat for *this* trip, pineapple jewelry they already own |
| Trigger | Packing week, or vendor table on embarkation day |
| Buy unit | 2–4 singles for themselves + 6–10 pack to trade |
| Disqualifier | Closeted at home with zero event calendar; will not wear a tapable disc in public |

**Jobs they will pay $10 to finish:** walk the lido looking available without looking thirsty; hand something that is not a room number; keep the night after the pool closes without adding a stranger on Instagram.

### Secondary — Solo / unicorn / single male (25%)

Solo woman or vetted single male, 28–45, event-selected not bar-selected.

Job: look intentional, not predatory. A charm you *give* is safer than a phone you *ask for*.

Buy unit: 1 worn + 2–3 to gift. Price sensitivity higher. $10 is the ceiling, not the floor.

### Tertiary — House / host / takeover lead (15% of revenue, not units)

Party organizer, villa host, cruise social chair. Buys 50–200 at pack/wholesale. Uses beads as door favors + night graph. This is the $3.90 10-pack customer, not the $10 single.

Do not sell $10 singles as the house SKU. That fights the pack.

### Anti-ICP

- Vanilla cruise families who think pineapple = hospitality
- ENM theorists who want essays, not a disc
- Content-farm couples who only want an OnlyFans link-in-bio NFC (different product; cheapens the room)
- Minors / 18–20 festival crowd (hard age-gate, event-only distribution)

### Core job

**When** we meet someone we actually want at a lifestyle event,  
**we want to** exchange a durable, low-awkward handle  
**so we can** decide later — sober, with our partner, off the deck — without losing the night.

### Job map

| Stage | Current hack | Failure | TapThat job |
|---|---|---|---|
| Signal | Pineapple pin, black ring, color beads | Signal ≠ contact | Charm is signal *and* endpoint |
| Approach | “Are you in the lifestyle?” | High false-positive, social cost | Tap is opt-in; no speech required |
| Exchange | Room number, IG, SDC handle yelled over music | Wet phones, fake handles, partner veto later | Disc leaves with them; profile waits |
| After | Scroll 400 Feeld likes from 12 miles away | Context died | Charm history = *this* night, *this* person |
| Keep | Group chat dump | Noise, screenshots, drama | Public/private + pass-it-on log |

### Functional jobs

1. Wear a coded, event-native object that reads as jewelry, not a lanyard.
2. Transfer identity in under 5 seconds, one-handed, wet.
3. Couple-safe: one disc, two humans, later veto.
4. Trade as a game (collect moments), not as a hookup form.
5. Work on iPhone NFC bar + Android mid-back without an app preinstall.

### Emotional jobs

- Feel chosen, not scanned.
- Keep plausible deniability at breakfast.
- Reduce “did we miss them?” regret on debarkation morning.
- Give the shy partner a prop so the bold partner does not run the whole night.

### Social jobs

- Look like you belong on *this* ship, not like you brought a startup.
- Hosts look generous (hand a charm, not a flyer).
- Couples look coordinated (matching discs beat matching shirts).

### Forces

**Pushes:** wet phones; Feeld density dies at sea; SDC is homework; pineapple jewelry is mute; color-bead necklaces are cruise-operator locked.

**Pulls:** object they already understand (charm); tap is familiar from hotel keys; $10 is a drink.

**Anxieties:** “Does this dox us at home?” “Does tap log GPS?” “Will vanilla guests see the app?” “Is this an OnlyFans funnel?”

**Habits:** they already buy $18–35 Etsy pineapple jewelry that does nothing.

FAQ on-site already names location tracking. Lead with: **no GPS, no background radio, disc is dumb metal until someone chooses to open the link.**

## PMM

### Positioning

**For** lifestyle couples and solos on cruises, resorts, and pineapple weekends,  
**TapThat is** the charm you tap instead of the username you shout,  
**that** keeps the night after the pool closes.  
**Unlike** pineapple jewelry (signal, no handle) or Feeld/SDC (handle, no moment),  
**we** put the profile in the object they already want to wear and trade.

One line: **A disc you tap. A night you keep.**  
Do not “clarify” into swinger-speak on the public storefront.

### Message house

| Layer | Line | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Coded public | Tap that. Follow the rest. | Site, ads that can leak |
| Event | Trade it on the lido, at the palapa, or wherever the pineapple is pointing. | Product cards, packing emails |
| Job | You met them. Your phone is wet. The charm is the card. | How-it-works, vendor script |
| Couple | One tap. Two vetoes. Decide at the cabin. | Couple SKU, FAQ |
| Solo | Gift the disc. Don’t ask for the phone. | Unicorn / single landing |
| Host | 10-pack is the weekend move. | Wholesale, house kits |

**Words that convert in-room:** pineapple, lido, palapa, takeover, afters, pass it on, public/private, after the pool closes.

**Words that kill the room on the public site:** swinger, hotwife, full swap, DTF, OnlyFans. Save those for opted-in lists.

### $10 / bead — verdict

**$10 is the correct event-table single. It is the wrong DTC everyday price if the 10-pack stays $39.**

| SKU | Price | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Event single (the bead) | **$10** | Impulse next to a $16 cocktail. Undercuts live $16.50. Beats $18–35 mute Etsy pineapple jewelry on utility. |
| Worn pair (couple kit) | **$18** | Two discs, one bag. Default couple CTA. |
| Weekend 10-pack | **$39–49** | Keep near current $38.99. Hosts and traders. $3.90–$5/unit. |
| House brick (100) | **$3–4/unit** | Do not publish $10 here. |

**Why $10 works for this ICP**

- Event guests already spend thousands. $10 is not a decision; $16.50 starts to feel like merch markup.
- Competes with a second drink, not with Feeld annual.
- $10 is “I’ll take four to trade.” $16.50 is “I’ll take one.” Trading *is* the loop.
- Bliss-style color beads are free-with-loyalty. You cannot win “free.” You win **portable after the operator’s necklace comes off**.

**Why $10 fails if you only drop the homepage price**

- 10-pack at $39 makes the $10 single look like a sucker SKU.
- Frame it: **$10 is the one you keep on the bracelet. The pack is for the night.**

Margin (INFERRED; no COGS on site): enamel + NFC disc + jump ring is typically well under $3 landed at quantity. $10 event / $4 pack still prints. Custom shapes and hats are margin mixers, not the wedge.

### Offer architecture

1. **The one you wear** — $10 single. Default image: on a wet wrist, pineapple in frame.
2. **The couple kit** — $18. Two discs, “one tap, two vetoes.”
3. **The weekend move** — 10-pack $39. “Trade six. Keep the night.”
4. **House drop** — 100+ quoted. Commemorative night, branded disc.
5. **App** — free to activate. Do not subscription-gate first tap. Paid later: extra rooms, private albums, event graphs. Charging before first tap kills density.

### Objections

| Objection | Answer |
|---|---|
| Tracks me? | Disc has no battery and no GPS. Link opens only when someone chooses to. |
| Partner will see? | Public/private at activation. Cabin veto is the feature. |
| Vanilla guest taps it? | Profile can be event-coded and locked. Charm still looks like a pineapple. |
| My Android is picky. | Mid-back, unlocked home screen. Printed on the card in the bag. |
| Why not just Feeld? | Feeld is for Tuesday in Denver. This is for the person standing in the pool. |
| Why not Etsy pineapple? | That jewelry cannot open a profile at 1am. |

### Channels

Density over reach.

1. **In-room:** embarkation tables, villa check-in, pool-bar consignment. Highest conversion. $10 cash / tap-to-pay.
2. **Host seeding:** 10 free to every takeover lead who will hand them at the door. Density beats ads.
3. **Packing-list affiliates:** lifestyle travel agents, opted-in cruise groups, Desire/Hedonism chats. 20% on couple kits.
4. **DTC:** current site. Keep coded. Paid social only against interest stacks that already buy pineapple jewelry / Desire / Bliss. Expect leak-to-vanilla; coded copy only.
5. **Do not:** TikTok explainers that decode the pineapple for normies. That burns the signal and the ICP.

### 30-day narrative

- Week 0: cut single to $10 on event one-pagers; leave site 10-pack at $39.
- Week 1: 20 host kits (10-packs) on the next 5 named events from the homepage ticker.
- Week 2: couple-kit landing with the veto line. No new features.
- Week 3: measure tap → activate → second-chapter rate, not ROAS.
- Kill if activation <40% of taps or second-chapter <15% — then the object is jewelry, not a network.

### Metrics

- Taps / disc / night
- Activate rate
- Couple-linked profiles
- Second chapter written (the product working)
- 10-pack attach on singles
- Repeat event repurchase

Vanity to ignore: followers, “brand awareness,” homepage sessions from vanilla pineapple curiosity.

## Call

TapThat is already aimed at this ICP. The site is doing the hard part: **speak lifestyle without saying lifestyle.**

$10/bead is the right **physical impulse price** for solos and couples *on property*. It is not a rebrand and not a Feeld killer. Sell the missing object in a stack they already pay for: signal jewelry + after-party handle.
